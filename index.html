Mac mini rental

Yifan Jiang · sole trader, London, United Kingdom

We rent out dedicated Mac mini computers on a monthly basis. Each machine is used by one customer only and is accessed remotely over the internet, so you get a full macOS desktop without buying the hardware. Typical uses include software development and testing, building and signing apps, and everyday office work. No physical goods are shipped.

How it works

  1. Choose a plan and pay online. Payments are processed securely by Stripe; we accept major cards and Alipay.
  2. We prepare your Mac and email you the connection details, usually within 1 business day.
  3. Connect with Screen Sharing (from a Mac), VNC (from Windows) or SSH.
  4. Your rental period starts when the machine is delivered and ready to use.

Terms of service

  • Each machine is rented to a single customer and may not be resold, shared publicly or sub-let.
  • You must use the machine lawfully. You may not use it to run proxy or VPN services for others, send spam, attack other systems, mine cryptocurrency, or store or distribute illegal content.
  • We may suspend a machine that is used in breach of these terms. Where possible we will contact you first.
  • When a rental ends, access stops and the machine is erased. Please back up your data before the end date.
  • We aim for 99% monthly availability. If a machine is unavailable for longer because of a fault on our side, we extend your rental by the same amount of time.

Refund policy

You can request a refund within 7 days of the machine being delivered. We refund the amount paid minus the time already used, charged pro rata at the monthly rate. After 7 days, payments for the current rental period are non-refundable. To request a refund, email us with your order details.

Privacy

We only collect what we need to provide the service: your name, email address and payment details (handled by Stripe). We do not access the contents of your machine except when you ask us to help, and we do not sell your data.
