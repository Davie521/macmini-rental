Questions

If something is not covered here, just email us.

Will the Mac be shipped to me?

No. The machine stays with us and you use it over the internet. Nothing physical is sent.

Is the machine shared with anyone else?

No. Each Mac mini is rented to one customer at a time and is erased between customers.

Can I connect from Windows?

Yes. Use any VNC client, such as the free TigerVNC, or connect by SSH from a terminal.

What happens when my rental ends?

Access stops at the end of the paid period and the machine is erased. Please back up anything you want to keep before then. Renew before the end date to keep your setup.

Can I get a refund?

Yes, within 7 days of delivery. See the refund policy below.

Terms of service

  • Each machine is rented to a single customer and may not be resold, sub-let or shared publicly.
  • Use the machine lawfully. You may not run proxy or VPN services for others, send spam, attack other systems, mine cryptocurrency, or store or distribute illegal content.
  • We may suspend a machine used in breach of these terms. Where possible we will contact you first.
  • Access ends when the rental period ends, and the machine is then erased.
  • If a machine is unavailable because of a fault on our side, we extend your rental by the same amount of time.

Refund policy

You can request a refund within 7 days of the machine being delivered. We refund the amount paid minus the time already used, charged pro rata at the monthly rate. After 7 days, payment for the current period is non-refundable. To request a refund, email us with your order details.

Privacy

We collect only what we need to run the service: your name, email address and payment details, which are handled by Stripe. We do not look at the contents of your machine unless you ask us to help, and we never sell your data.
